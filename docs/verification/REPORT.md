## First automatic run — FAILED quality (kept as evidence: `first-run-classification.txt`)
- GothamChess 12/12 → "Puzzle & Math"; OverSimplified → Puzzle & Math / Chatgpt & Game / Election & Math;
  Adam Ragusea 1 → "Machine Learning & Statistics"; Steve Mould mostly → "Number & Prime".
- Kurzgesagt health videos created a subniche "Cancer" **under "Neural Network"**.

**Root causes (measured, not guessed):**
1. Raw MiniLM cosines share a large common component: unrelated chess/history videos scored 0.46–0.54 against science
   categories, overlapping real members (p10 0.52). Mean-centered, real members p10 0.31 / median 0.53, chess/history ≤ 0.28.
2. Small groups without a fitting parent were forced under the nearest niche.
3. After centering, a just-created niche looked like a parent to any other non-science group (history 0.35, health 0.33).
   Existing subniches score median 0.70 vs their own parent and at most 0.40 vs any other parent.
4. Channel confidence divided by *classified* videos only (Kurzgesagt → Image = 1.0 from 1 of 12 videos).

**Fixes** (`src/server/services/taxonomy/index.ts`): incremental decisions compare corpus-mean-centered vectors
(reuse ≥ 0.32, pool grouping ≥ 0.45, parent ≥ 0.45, duplicate ≥ 0.80); a group with no fitting parent and fewer than
10 videos stays pooled; channel share uses all of the channel's videos. Regression tests added. The 72 videos were
reclassified with the new `pnpm taxonomy reclassify --channels …` (already-collected data only; no API calls).
